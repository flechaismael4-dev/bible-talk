S.T.R.E.E.T.

Compassion in action.

STRIVING TO RESTORE, EMPOWER, EVERYONE TOGETHER

S.T.R.E.E.T. is the hands-on outreach expression of this mission, serving people experiencing chronic homelessness and others facing difficult circumstances.

  • Respectful, person-centered engagement
  • Practical supplies and basic necessities
  • Encouragement, connection, and referrals
  • Sharing the hope of the Gospel
